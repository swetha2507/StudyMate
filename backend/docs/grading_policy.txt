Course Grading Policy
Late submissions are accepted up to 48 hours past the due date with a 10% deduction. 
Assignments submitted later than this window will not be graded. 
Make-up exams will be offered only for documented emergencies.

Final grades are based on the following weights:
- Assignments: 40%
- Quizzes: 20%
- Midterm Exam: 20%
- Final Project: 20%
Participation may be considered when determining borderline grades.
