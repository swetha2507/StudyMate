RIT Academic Integrity Policy
All students are expected to uphold the highest standards of academic honesty. 
Cheating, plagiarism, and unauthorized collaboration are violations that may result 
in academic penalties or disciplinary action. 

Students who are unsure whether an action constitutes a violation should consult 
their instructor before submitting the work. Repeat violations may lead to suspension 
or expulsion. Appeals can be filed through the Office of Student Conduct.
